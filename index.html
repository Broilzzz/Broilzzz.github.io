Questions

Good to know

Is Party Pop free?

Yes. Party Pop is free to download, and every game can be played on one phone for free, with an ad between rounds. Party Pass removes the ads. To play on several phones, one person hosts a room with Party Pass or by watching a short ad, and joining is always free.

How many players can play?

Party Pop is made for 2 to 6 players. Tic-Tac-Toe and Four in a Row are for two, or you can play them solo against a bot.

Can everyone play on their own phone?

Yes. In Local Multiplayer one person hosts a room and everyone else joins from their own phone on the same Wi-Fi network. If a phone drops out, its seat is kept while it reconnects.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up or login. Type in the players’ names and start playing.

Which devices does Party Pop run on?

iPhone and iPad from the App Store, and Android phones and tablets from Google Play.
